## What to do and which workflow to follow

Maintain specifications within the requested scope. Read applicable instructions and relevant documents, inspect affected references, make the smallest authorized update, review consistency and links, and report changes, checks and open questions. Follow the [repository workflow](../../AGENTS.md) and [specification skill](../skills/manage-specification/SKILL.md).

## What to consider during execution

Describe the final desired state. Keep requirements concise, numbered and consistent, with one authoritative location per rule. Follow the [specification conventions](../../specification/AGENTS.md). Distinguish confirmed decisions from assumptions and unresolved mappings; treat code, legacy SQL and samples as evidence rather than authority.

## Limitations

Change specifications only when explicitly authorized. Do not change application code, tests or runtime configuration, run deployments, invent business rules, or rewrite requirements to match implementation. Keep migration narratives and implementation details out of specifications; report conflicts and implementation gaps separately.
